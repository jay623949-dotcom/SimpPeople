Research Study Consent

This extension records the posts that appear in your Instagram home feed to study what the platform shows to users.

What is collected

  • Post caption, media type (image, video, carousel, reel), link, and whether it is an ad
  • The order and time in which posts appeared in your feed
  • Post authors, stored only as a pseudonymous code (not the username)

What is NOT collected

  • Your name, email, password, direct messages, stories, or other websites
  • Anything outside the Instagram home feed

Your rights

  • Pause, export, or delete your data anytime from the extension popup
  • Withdraw from the study at any time

Thank you!

You can close this tab. Use the extension icon to pause or delete your data anytime.
